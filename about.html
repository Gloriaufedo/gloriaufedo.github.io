About me

A scientist by training, an analyst by practice.

I work at the intersection of business, data and scientific research — turning complex datasets into analysis that people can actually use.

The short version

I like figuring out what the data is really saying.

My background started in Biochemistry, but my work has increasingly moved toward data analysis, business intelligence and computational research.

In professional settings, I have worked with sales, customer, marketing and operations data — using SQL, Power BI, Excel and Python to clean information, investigate questions, build reports and communicate findings.

I am particularly interested in the part of analytics that happens before the dashboard: understanding the problem, questioning the data, choosing the right method and making sure the resulting insight is actually useful.

What I bring
SQL Power BI Data Modeling Excel Python Statistical Analysis Business Reporting Research

I am comfortable moving from raw or relational data through cleaning and analysis to a clear report, dashboard or research finding.

How I work

I don't start with the chart.

Good analysis starts with a good question. My workflow usually moves through four stages.

01

Understand

Define the business or research question and understand what the available data can actually answer.

02

Prepare

Inspect, clean, transform and structure the data before using it for analysis.

03

Analyse

Use SQL, statistical methods, Python or BI tools depending on the question and the data.

04

Communicate

Turn the results into dashboards, reports or findings that are clear enough to support a decision.

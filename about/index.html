About me

Trained as a biochemist, working as a data analyst.

I work across business analytics and scientific research, turning complex datasets into analysis that decision-makers can use.

The short version

I start by questioning what the data can answer.

I started in Biochemistry and moved toward data analysis, business intelligence and computational research.

In professional roles, I have worked with sales, customer, marketing and operations data, using SQL, Power BI, Excel and Python to clean data, investigate questions, build reports and communicate findings.

I am most interested in the work that happens before the dashboard: understanding the problem, questioning the data and choosing the right method so the result is useful.

What I bring
SQL Power BI Data Modeling Excel Python Statistical Analysis Business Reporting Research

I take data from raw or relational form through cleaning and analysis to a finished report, dashboard or research finding.

How I work

The question comes before the chart.

My workflow has four stages.

01

Understand

Define the business or research question and check what the available data can answer.

02

Prepare

Inspect, clean, transform and structure the data before analysis.

03

Analyze

Use SQL, statistical methods, Python or BI tools, depending on the question and the data.

04

Communicate

Turn results into dashboards, reports or findings that support a decision.

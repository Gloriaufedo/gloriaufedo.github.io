RISK & INVESTIGATIVE ANALYTICS · PYTHON

Bitcoin Transaction Risk & Compliance Analysis

Question: Which transaction structures should a compliance team investigate first?

203K+ transactions 49 time steps 5,459-node subgraph

I built a transaction graph from the Elliptic Bitcoin Transaction Dataset and combined network measures into a structural risk score.

The forensic subgraph contained 5,459 nodes and 6,478 edges. The investigation identified 11 same-step layering flags and 4 fan-out patterns.

The risk score combines betweenness centrality, PageRank, out-degree and in-degree. It indicates structural risk patterns and is not proof of criminal activity or confirmed wallet identities.

MARKET & PRODUCT RESEARCH · HNG TEAM PROJECT

FitCall.me: Market Research & Product Opportunity Analysis

Role: Assistant Team Lead & Data Analyst, HNG Internship

Question: Is there enough demand for online accountability coaching, and which keywords, users and channels should the product go after first?

US & UK data $295.10B market by 2033 Team project (HNG)

I worked with a cross-functional product team to research market demand and growth opportunities for FitCall.me, a personal trainer app that connects fitness clients with trainers.

  • Supported team coordination and project execution as Assistant Team Lead.
  • Managed the project workflow through Kanban board coordination and task tracking.
  • Investigated search demand, keyword opportunities and content strategies across the US and UK.
  • Evaluated competitor positioning and gaps around pricing and scheduled live accountability.
  • Helped define priority client and trainer segments to guide positioning and acquisition.
  • Worked on the conditions that would show the current approach was failing and the signals that would justify a pivot.

Search data across the US and UK showed strong commercial demand for “online fitness coach”, and UK search interest in remote personal training rose 414% year over year. The global virtual fitness market is projected to reach $295.10 billion by 2033. Structured fitness programs see a 50% drop-out rate, and 80% of January gym joiners quit within five months.

Future is priced high, Trainwell and FlexIt do not focus on scheduled live accountability sessions, and no product pages ranked for “fitness accountability coach”. Clients were defined as busy professionals aged 25–45 in the US and UK. Trainers were defined as certified Nigerian fitness professionals seeking higher-paying international clients.

Recommendation: prioritize long-form SEO content targeting lower-competition, problem-aware searches, followed by high-intent paid landing pages and social awareness campaigns.

RISK & INVESTIGATIVE ANALYTICS · PYTHON

Bitcoin Transaction Risk & Compliance Analysis

Question: Which transaction structures should a compliance team investigate first?

203K+ transactions 49 time steps 5,459-node subgraph

I constructed a transaction graph from the Elliptic Bitcoin Transaction Dataset and combined network measures to create a structural risk score.

The forensic subgraph contained 5,459 nodes and 6,478 edges. The investigation identified 11 same-step layering flags and 4 fan-out patterns.

The risk score combines Betweenness Centrality, PageRank, Out-Degree and In-Degree. These scores indicate structural risk patterns; they are not proof of criminal activity or confirmed wallet identities.

DATA WAREHOUSE · SQL

Palladium Bank — Retail Banking Data Warehouse

Question: How can raw banking transaction logs be structured into a reusable analytical layer?

18 months of transactions 7 dimensions Transaction-grain fact table

I designed a dimensional model around customer, account, branch, product, channel, transaction type and date dimensions, supported by a central fact_transactions table.

The customer dimension uses SCD Type 2 to preserve customer-tier history, while the branch dimension uses SCD Type 1.

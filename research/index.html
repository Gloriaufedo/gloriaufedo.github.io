TRANSCRIPTOMICS · FERROPTOSIS

Erastin & Ferrostatin RNA-seq Analysis in HepG2 Cells

Question: How does ferroptosis induction alter gene expression, and how does treatment with Ferrostatin compare with the control profile?

GEO: GSE104462 3 groups 3 samples/group

I analyzed expression data from Control, Erastin-treated and Ferrostatin-treated HepG2 cells. The workflow included log transformation, gene-wise OLS regression, multiple-testing correction and pathway enrichment.

The 30 genes with the smallest adjusted p-values in the Erastin analysis were selected for hierarchical clustering. Of these, 15 had positive and 15 had negative expression effects relative to Control.

In the PCA, PC1 explained 64.2% of the variance and PC2 explained 18.9%, together accounting for 83.1%.

CANCER GENOMICS · SURVIVAL ANALYSIS

Ferroptosis Biomarker Discovery in TCGA-LIHC

Question: Is GPX4 expression associated with tumor status and overall survival in liver hepatocellular carcinoma?

TCGA-LIHC RNA-seq + clinical data Survival analysis

I used RNA-seq data and clinical metadata from the GDC API to examine GPX4 expression and its relationship with patient survival.

The analysis compared tumor and normal expression and applied Kaplan–Meier survival analysis with log-rank testing, stratifying patients at median expression.

GPX4 expression was significantly higher in tumor tissue (p < 0.001), and higher expression was associated with poorer overall survival over the follow-up period examined (p < 0.05).

IMMUNOLOGY · SURVIVAL ANALYSIS

Immune Microenvironment Analysis in TCGA-LIHC

Question: Is estimated CD8 T-cell activity associated with patient survival after accounting for clinical factors?

TCGA-LIHC ssGSEA Cox regression

I used single-sample gene set enrichment analysis to estimate immune-cell enrichment, then examined how CD8 enrichment relates to overall survival.

The multivariable model adjusted for age and AJCC stage and treated CD8 enrichment as a continuous variable.

CD8 enrichment was not significant in the univariable Kaplan–Meier analysis (p = 0.120), but the adjusted Cox model gave a hazard ratio of 0.61 with p = 0.04.

SINGLE-CELL RNA-SEQ · IMMUNOLOGY

Single-Cell RNA-seq Analysis of Human PBMCs

Question: Can unsupervised analysis distinguish major immune-cell populations in human PBMC data?

2,700 cells 32,738 genes 10x count matrix

I converted a 10x count matrix into an AnnData object, applied cell-level quality control, normalized the data and identified highly variable genes. I then ran PCA, built a neighborhood graph and applied Leiden clustering.

UMAP visualization and canonical marker expression guided cluster interpretation. Markers included CD3D and CD8A for T cells, NKG7 for NK cells, MS4A1 for B cells and LYZ for monocytes.

Differential expression between clusters was assessed with the Wilcoxon rank-sum test.
